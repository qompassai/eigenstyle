AI models meeting these alignment criteria will ﬁnd universal use whereas misaligned models will face appropriate, corrective action. Dr. Geoffrey Hinton, the “godfather of AI,” built the neural networks of deep learning in pursuit of understanding how the human brain works [1, 10]. Deep learning training emulates the synaptic plasticity and long-term potentiation observed with learning in the human brain. Generative AI large language models (LLMs) emerged following this process. Data quality, training time, and neural network architecture drive LLM performance, domain speciﬁcity, and data modality. Early LLMs built with convolutional neural networks and recurrent neural networks found limited use outside industry and academia because of computational requirements [6]. Deep learning methodology

The multiheaded self-attention mechanism of the transformer neural network architecture changed that paradigm in 2017 [5]. Transformer LLMs performed natural language processing through proliﬁc parallel sequencing with outputs reﬂecting contextual understanding not seen with convolutional neural networks and recurrent neural networks. The self-attention mechanism sparked the invention of new architectures and adaptation of convolutional neural networks and recurrent neural networks. The March 2023 leak of Frontier Large Language Model Meta AI (LLaMA) prompted further innovation from the global community [7]. Less than a year after ChatGPT’s release, large multimodal models like GPT-4(V) and a consistent stream of groundbreaking publications freely available from the National Institutes of Health endorsed eprint server arxiv.org as the new normal for the collective scientiﬁc community [7-9].
